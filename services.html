Learn it

Plumbing & Electrical: Learn It With Seth

Utah law doesn't allow handymen to replace plumbing or electrical fixtures. As the homeowner, you're allowed to do that work on your own home. So we bring the tools and the know-how, and Seth walks you through it step by step, in person, while you do the work.

  • Faucet replacement
  • Toilet replacement
  • Light fixture and ceiling fan swaps
  • Outlet and switch cover swaps
