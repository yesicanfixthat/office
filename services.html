DoorsPrimary specialty

Doors & Windows

Doors that stick, drag, or let the weather in. We adjust, repair, and upgrade interior doors and seal up the gaps around doors and windows.

  • Sticking and misaligned doors
  • Door hardware installation
  • Casing upgrades
  • Weather stripping replacement
  • Door thresholds
  • Screen repairs

  • Painting and touch-ups
